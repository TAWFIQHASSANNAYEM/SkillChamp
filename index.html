Why SkillChamp

Built to evaluate employability, not just academic results.

The Graduate Section follows the official campus execution kit. Teams are judged on real-world soft skills, practical thinking, and how well they can present a meaningful solution in the Bangladesh context.

GPA, accent, and flashy slides are not the priority. Judges look for clarity, teamwork, empathy, feasibility, and authentic communication.

Eligibility & Rules

What every campus team needs to know before competing.

See Full Rules

Team Structure

  • Exactly 3 members from the university-level graduate section.
  • All members must be registered on Connectiya before the event.
  • All 3 members must join the presentation segment.

Scoring Logic

  • Aptitude scores are averaged across all 3 team members.
  • Presentation scores are judge-averaged and reward originality.
  • Courses and profile completion are verified before competition day.

Judge Focus

  • English perfection is not the benchmark; clarity matters more.
  • Teams should avoid plagiarism and copied startup ideas.
  • Judges reward feasibility, empathy, and thoughtful teamwork.
