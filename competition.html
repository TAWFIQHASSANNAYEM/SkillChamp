Official Campus Round Guide

Competition format, judging logic, and execution flow for the Graduate Section.

This page follows the official campus round execution kit so organizers, judges, and teams can understand exactly how SkillChamp 2026 should run.

Structure

One campus round. One 3-member team. One final score out of 100.

Team Rule

Exactly 3 university students

Segment Count

4 evaluated segments + 2 verified bonuses

Presentation Theme

SDG-based Bangladesh problem solving

Judge Principle

Skill, clarity, empathy, and feasibility matter most

Marks Breakdown

The full campus round scoring model.

Segment 1

Aptitude Test

35 marks

35 MCQs in 30 minutes. Each member sits individually, then the three scores are averaged into the team score.

Segment 2

Problem-Solving Presentation

30 marks

All 3 members must contribute. Teams present a practical SDG-linked solution relevant to Bangladesh.

Segment 3

Verbal Communication

20 marks

A representative or rotated participant speaks for 2 to 3 minutes on a random topic after a short prep time.

Segment 4

Judges Q&A

5 marks

Judges test presence of mind, answer relevance, and confidence under pressure.

Verified Bonus

Connectiya Courses

5 marks

Pre-event verification based on course completion level and quiz participation.

Verified Bonus

Complete Profile

5 marks

Profile information, education, skills, CV completeness, and professional presentation are checked before the event.

Aptitude Segment

35-question written test with no negative marking.

Format

  • 30 minutes duration.
  • 1 mark for each correct answer.
  • No negative marking.
  • Can be conducted online or with printed sheets.

Question Distribution

  • Verbal Reasoning: 5
  • Quantitative Aptitude: 5
  • General Knowledge: 5
  • Connectiya soft skills topics: 20
Presentation Segment

The most important part of the competition.

What teams must cover

  • Problem identification and who it affects.
  • Why the problem matters in real life.
  • Existing solution gaps.
  • The team's proposed solution.
  • Expected impact and SDG linkage.
  • Feasibility, sustainability, and team reflection.

Rubric overview

Problem Selection & SDG Alignment

How clearly the issue is tied to a real SDG challenge.

Solution Quality & Innovation

Whether the idea is practical, original, and root-cause focused.

Leadership & Teamwork

All 3 members should contribute meaningfully.

Emotional Intelligence & Empathy

Human understanding and social impact awareness.

Feasibility & Sustainability

Can it actually work and scale in the Bangladesh context?

Presentation & Delivery

Clear structure and confidence without accent bias.

Verbal Communication

20 marks

One participant speaks after drawing a topic. Judges score fluency, clarity, structure, and confidence.

  • 2 minutes preparation time.
  • No written notes during prep.
  • 2 to 3 minute delivery window.
  • Natural speaking is preferred over memorized speeches.
Judges Q&A

5 marks

After the presentation, judges ask 2 or 3 short questions to test real thinking, confidence, and answer relevance.

  • Any team member may answer.
  • No preparation time is given.
  • Quick thinking and relevance matter more than long answers.
Organizer Checklist

Must-do items before competition day.

Confirm all teams have exactly 3 members and are registered on Connectiya.
Verify Connectiya profile completion and course engagement before the event.
Pre-fill team names and university names in the judging workbook.
Prepare judges, the topic draw box, projector, and aptitude logistics.
Use at least 2 judges for presentation scoring.
Keep judge scoring independent until every team has been marked.
